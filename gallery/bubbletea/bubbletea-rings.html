How to read it

Ring width = commits that month (√ scaled).
Dark band = share of lines deleted. Pruning shows as dense latewood.
Lopsided growth = where the code grew: each direction around the trunk is one folder (labels on the bark).
Thin dark line = a month with no commits.
Scar = a month that deleted far more than it added.
Resin bead = a tag. Big beads are x.0.0 / 0.x.0 releases.
Ray = a top contributor, from their first ring to their last.

Lockfiles, build output and snapshots are not counted.
