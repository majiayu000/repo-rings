Repositories, read like trees

Each cross-section is one repository's whole history: the pith is the first commit, the bark is HEAD, and every ring is a period of commits. Open one to watch it grow and to read any ring. Made with repo-rings; every field note cites a real tag or commit and was checked against git before rendering.
